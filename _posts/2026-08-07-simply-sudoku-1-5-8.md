This one's a small polish release — the kind of thing you feel more than notice.
Two little annoyances in the note-taking flow are gone, and there's the usual
behind-the-scenes tidying.

### What changed

- **Sticky pen turns off when you tap a filled cell.** Before, it could stay armed
  and surprise you on the next tap.
- **Highlights no longer stack.** Selecting a new cell clears the old same-number
  glow cleanly.
- Version bumped everywhere and re-verified on Windows and Android.

### Why it matters

Simply Sudoku is meant to get out of your way. When a control does something you
didn't ask for, that's friction — and friction is exactly what this whole project
exists to remove. Small fixes like these are most of what keeps an app pleasant
over the long haul.

As always: no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and it works fully offline. If you
enjoy it, a [donation](https://buy.stripe.com/dRm5kwawvd9L7oK2fu1B605) helps cover
the real costs.
