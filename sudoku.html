Themes and board looks Pro

Ten themes for the app around the board, and twelve looks for the board itself — or design your own. The free app has the Default and Dark themes and the Classic, Midnight and High-Contrast boards, and can preview the rest.

22 ways to play Pro

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone. Each kind has a daily puzzle of its own; the free app plays one game of each.

Learn as you play

A hint shows the next logical step in three stages, and counts once however far you read. Lessons practice one technique at a time; the Techniques page explains every step the hints use.

Ways to start a game

Today’s puzzle, a new game, the daily archive, collections, a code a friend sent, or a puzzle from a book. The sticky pen (hold a number until it lights up) fills the same digit wherever you tap.

Your games

Every game is kept: replay it move by move, see your statistics and your progress, earn achievements, and keep a certificate for a win worth remembering.

Make it yours

Save your favorite kind of game, play with letters or shapes, turn the sound and vibration on or off, pick one of ten languages and the text size. Landscape, tablets and Windows each get a layout of their own.

Questions

I play Simply Sudoku. How do I move?

Simply Sudoku’s last update has a Bring my games along button. Install Sudoku by ANA, tap that button in Simply Sudoku, then Settings → Import from Simply Sudoku in Sudoku by ANA. Your best times, statistics and unfinished game come along. (Simply Sudoku never kept a list of past games, so there is none to bring.) Simply Sudoku keeps working, but it won’t be updated again.

I bought Sudoku Pro by ANA. How do I bring my free games?

In Sudoku by ANA, tap Settings → Take my games to Pro and save the file; then in Sudoku Pro by ANA, Settings → Import. Every game, statistic and best time comes along. In Pro, Export my data and Import also move your games to a new phone.

Is Pro a subscription, or an in-app purchase?

Neither. Sudoku Pro by ANA is a separate app, paid once in the store. Nothing inside either app can be bought, and nothing expires.

What does the app know about me?

Nothing. It collects no data, asks for no permissions and never connects to the internet; your games stay on your device. Google Play’s Data safety says No data collected. The privacy policy has the details.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets; iPhone, iPad and Mac; and Windows. It plays in portrait on phones; Pro adds landscape. On a computer, every page works from the keyboard, and screen readers announce the selected cell.
