Features

Everything you want. Nothing you don’t.

One screen to start from

Sudoku by ANA opens on a start panel with every way to play: pick a level, continue the game you left off, take on today’s puzzle, deal a fresh game, or look back at your history. One tap and you’re solving.

Today’s puzzle — the same one for everyone

One puzzle per level per day that everyone gets, so you can compare times with a friend or make it part of your morning. Pro adds the daily archive: every past daily puzzle, back to the first one.

Notes, sticky numbers & hints on your terms

Pencil in candidates, hold a number to make it sticky, and take a hint only when you want one — then keep it or undo it. Every puzzle is verified to have exactly one solution, so logic alone always gets you there.

Killer and Samurai Pro

Killer: cages with sums and few or no givens at all. Samurai: five overlapping grids on one 21×21 board, with pinch to zoom. Plus Diagonal and Hyper, the 16×16 board, and every level up to Expert. Every one checked to have exactly one solution.

Cages, sums, no clutter Pro

Killer cages are drawn as quiet dashed outlines with the sum tucked in a corner, readable in every board look. Pencil marks move aside so they never sit under a sum.

Collections, codes and your history Pro

Symmetry, Extreme and a new Weekend Challenge every week. Type any word or number for a custom game, or share a short puzzle code so a friend gets the same board. The full history lets you replay or review any game — the free edition shows your last ten.

Statistics, achievements and certificates

Streaks and achievements in every edition. Pro adds the full page — trends, records, monthly and yearly summaries — achievement progress, and certificates and board pictures to keep or share.
