Tired of puzzle apps that interrupt every game with an ad? **Simply Sudoku is now Sudoku by ANA** — the same quiet
game, a new name, and version 2.0 with more to play than ever. Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and it never
needs the internet.

### What's in the free app
- **Today's puzzle**, the same for every player.
- **Hints that teach:** a nudge first, then the reasoning, then the move.
- **Lessons and a first-run guide** that walk through the techniques the hints use.
- **A game history**, achievements, best times and a calendar of the days you played.
- **NEW - MY DEFAULT:** your favorite kind of game, one tap away.
- **Ten languages:** English, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Italiano, 日本語, 简体中文, 한국어 and 繁體中文.
- Gentle sounds and, on phones, a light vibration, each with its own switch; full keyboard play on a computer and
  screen-reader support.
- Two games a day — today's puzzle included — and **one free game of each Pro puzzle kind** (TRY PRO).

### Sudoku Pro by ANA — bought once, no subscription
- Every level, from Easy to Expert, and unlimited games.
- **22 ways to play:** the 12x12 and 16x16 boards, Killer, Samurai, Jigsaw, Kropki, Twodoku, Girandola and more —
  **each with a daily puzzle of its own**.
- **Collections** graded by technique, the **daily archive**, every lesson.
- **Puzzle codes** (`9M-COFFEE`): share one, and anyone can play the same puzzle. Enter a puzzle from a book, too.
- Full statistics, every game in the history, **move-by-move replay**, your progress and your year in Sudoku.
- Every theme and board look, your own board looks, letters or shapes in place of digits, landscape, certificates,
  printing and backups between devices.

### Coming from Simply Sudoku
Sudoku by ANA is a new app in the store, but your record comes with you: Simply Sudoku's last update has a
**Bring my games along** button. Tap it there, then **Settings → Import from Simply Sudoku** in Sudoku by ANA
(**Settings → Import** in Pro), and your best times, statistics and unfinished game arrive. (Simply Sudoku never
kept a list of past games, so there is none to bring.) Moving from the free app to Pro later is just as easy:
**Settings → Take my games to Pro** in Sudoku by ANA, then **Import** in Sudoku Pro by ANA.
