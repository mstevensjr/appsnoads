Tired of puzzle apps that interrupt every game with an ad? **Simply Sudoku is now Sudoku by ANA** — the same quiet
game, a new name, and version 2.0 with more to play than ever. Still no ads, no accounts, no tracking, and it never
needs the internet.

### What's new for everyone
- **Today's puzzle**, the same for every player, at every level.
- **Hints that teach:** the next logical step, a nudge first, then the reasoning, then the move.
- **Lessons and a first-run guide** that walk through the techniques the hints use.
- **A game history**, achievements and best times.
- **Six languages:** English, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Italiano.
- **Help built in** (About → Help), full keyboard play on a computer, and a board that tells TalkBack and VoiceOver
  which cell is selected.
- The free edition plays two games a day — today's puzzle included — and one free game of each Pro puzzle.

### Sudoku Pro by ANA — bought once, no subscription
- Every level, from Easy to Expert, and the **16x16** board.
- **Six variants:** Diagonal, Hyper, Killer, Samurai, Jigsaw and Mini 6x6 — **each with a daily puzzle of its own**.
- **Puzzle codes:** every game has one (`9M-COFFEE`); share it, and anyone can play the same puzzle.
- **Collections**, the **daily archive**, full statistics and history, certificates, every theme and board look.

### Coming from Simply Sudoku
Sudoku by ANA is a new app in the stores, so it starts with a fresh history; Simply Sudoku keeps yours for as long as
it stays installed, and its last update points to the new apps. From here on, moving is easy: **Settings → Export my
data** in Sudoku by ANA, then **Import** in Sudoku Pro by ANA (or on a new phone) brings every game along.
