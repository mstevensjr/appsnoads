How it plays

The crossword, the way you like it.

Set the puzzle up your way

Four levels — Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert — decide how common the words and how direct the clues are. Choose a quick 7×7 or a fuller 11×11 grid, and how dense you want it. Tap New and the grid is filled and clued in a moment, on the device, from the built-in dictionary.

Clues that follow you

Tap a cell and its clue appears beneath the grid with the whole word highlighted; tap a clue in the list and the grid jumps to it. The clue list scrolls alongside, ‹ › step through the clues, and when you finish a word the cursor moves on to the next unfinished one. Type on the on-screen keyboard or a real one.

Help on your terms

Auto-check marks a wrong letter as you type — or switch it off and solve blind, then press ✓ Check when the grid is full. Stuck? Reveal a letter, a word, or the whole puzzle. A timer runs while you solve and pauses when you do.

An honest, open dictionary

The words and clues come from open, permissively licensed sources — the SCOWL word lists, the WordNet® lexical database from Princeton University, and the Crossword Nexus and Matt Abate crossword word lists — merged into one offline database that ships inside the app. Credits are in the app’s About screen, and nothing is fetched from the internet, ever.

Free and Pro

Crosswords is free. Crosswords Pro is paid once.

The free edition makes unlimited puzzles — no ads, no accounts, nothing to unlock inside the app. Crosswords Pro is a separate edition on the same stores: one small payment, no subscription, no in-app purchase, and it keeps the free edition free.

The promise

No ads. No accounts. No tracking.

Crosswords works completely offline: the dictionary is on the device, the puzzles are made on the device, and your progress and settings stay on the device. It requests no permissions at all.
