Privacy Policy

The short version: this app collects nothing.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In plain English: Apps No Ads apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information. They contain no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers, and they work fully offline. Anything you create in an app stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Apps No Ads — the software label of Melvin Stevens — handles information in its applications and on this website (appsnoads.com). It applies to Sudoku by ANA and Sudoku Pro by ANA (formerly Simply Sudoku and Simply Sudoku Pro) and to all other Apps No Ads apps.

1. Information collected

None. These apps do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information. There is no account to create and no sign-in. No advertising identifiers are used, and the apps ask for no device permissions.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the apps work, some information is saved locally on your device only and is never transmitted to Apps No Ads or anyone else. For Sudoku by ANA and Sudoku Pro by ANA this includes:

  • Your current game and puzzle progress (so an unfinished game can be offered back on the next launch);
  • Your game history — each game’s kind, level, time and result, and the moves of recent and starred games so they can be replayed;
  • Your best completion times and statistics — wins, losses, streaks, times, mistakes and hints — and your achievements and progress;
  • App settings and preferences, including your default game and any board looks you design;
  • In the free app, the dates of the day’s games (for its two games a day) and which free Pro games you have tried;
  • A few counters for the rating prompt — how many games you’ve won and launches you’ve made, when the app last asked you to rate it, and whether you’ve rated or opted out — so it can avoid asking too often.

This data stays in the app’s private storage on your device. None of it identifies you, and none of it is transmitted. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app; the Reset statistics button in the app clears the statistics.

Sudoku by ANA and Sudoku Pro by ANA are the same app in two editions. Both keep their data locally; neither shares it with the other on Android or iOS, where each app is sandboxed. A backup file (Export my data in Pro, Take my games to Pro in the free app) is made only when you ask, and goes only where you choose to save or share it. Importing one, or Simply Sudoku’s Bring my games along hand-off, reads it on your device; nothing is uploaded.

3. No ads, analytics, or tracking

These apps contain no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no social-media or third-party tracking libraries. No user profiles are built, and there is nothing to sell to third parties, because nothing is collected.

4. No internet connection required

Sudoku by ANA and Sudoku Pro by ANA are designed to run fully offline. It does not send data over the network during normal use. The only times it hands off to another app are ones you start yourself:

  • Rate / Write a review / Upgrade to Pro open the app’s page in your device’s app store (or, where no store page exists yet, this website) in the store app or your browser. What happens there is governed by that platform’s privacy policy (see section 7).
  • Send feedback opens a new message in your own mail app, addressed to Apps No Ads, containing only what you typed plus the app’s name, edition and version. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it from your mail app, and the game itself makes no network connection.
  • Share code, certificates, board pictures and Print hand what you chose to your device’s share sheet or print dialog; nothing leaves the device unless you send it on.
  • On iOS, the app may occasionally show Apple’s built-in rating prompt after a notable win. That prompt is provided and controlled by the operating system; Apps No Ads receives nothing from it and cannot see what you chose.

5. Buying the author a coffee, and buying Pro

If you choose to buy the author a coffee, the payment is processed by Stripe, a third-party payment provider, through a secure Stripe checkout page. Apps No Ads never sees or stores your card details. Any information you provide there is handled under Stripe’s Privacy Policy. Donations are optional and are not required to use any app.

If you buy Sudoku Pro by ANA (or Simply Sudoku Pro before it), the purchase is made through and processed by the app store you buy it from (Google Play, the Apple App Store, or the Microsoft Store) under that store’s terms and privacy policy. Apps No Ads receives no payment details and no personal information about the buyer. There are no purchases inside either edition of the app.

6. This website

This website is a set of static pages and loads no third-party fonts. Temporarily, and only to help promote the launch of its apps, the site includes Google's advertising tag (Google Ads / gtag.js). It is used solely to measure how many visitors arrive from our ads and go on to install the app, so we can gauge whether that promotion is working. We retain no user-specific data from it and build no personal profiles — it exists only for aggregate campaign measurement, and this tag will be removed once the launch promotion ends. The web host may also keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliability; these are not used to identify or track you.

To be clear, this applies to the website only: the apps themselves — including Sudoku by ANA and Sudoku Pro by ANA — still contain no ads, no analytics, and no trackers of any kind (see section 3), and continue to work fully offline.

7. App store platforms

The app stores that distribute these apps (Google Play, the Apple App Store, and the Microsoft Store) may collect their own information — for example, download counts or aggregate statistics — under their own privacy policies. That collection is controlled by those platforms, not by Apps No Ads.

8. Children’s privacy

These apps are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above revised. Because no personal data is collected, individual notifications aren’t possible.

10. Contact
