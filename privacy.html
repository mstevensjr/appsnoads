Privacy Policy

The short version: we don’t collect anything.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

In plain English: Apps No Ads apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information. They contain no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers, and they work fully offline. Anything you create in an app stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Apps No Ads (“we”, “us”) handles information in our applications and on this website (appsnoads.com). It applies to Simple Sudoku and our other apps.

1. Information we collect

None. Our apps do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information. There is no account to create and no sign-in. We do not use advertising identifiers.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the apps work, some information is saved locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. For Simple Sudoku this includes:

  • Your current game and puzzle progress;
  • Your best completion times per difficulty;
  • App settings and preferences.

This data stays in the app’s private storage on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

3. No ads, analytics, or tracking

Our apps contain no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no social-media or third-party tracking libraries. We do not build profiles of users and we have nothing to sell to third parties, because we collect nothing.

4. No internet connection required

Simple Sudoku is designed to run fully offline. It does not send data over the network during normal use.

5. Donations

If you choose to make a voluntary donation, the payment is processed by Stripe, a third-party payment provider, through a secure Stripe checkout page. We never see or store your card details. Any information you provide there is handled under Stripe’s Privacy Policy. Donations are optional and are not required to use any app.

6. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads no third-party fonts or trackers. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliability; these are not used to identify or track you.

7. App store platforms

The app stores that distribute our apps (Google Play, the Apple App Store, and the Microsoft Store) may collect their own information — for example, download counts or aggregate statistics — under their own privacy policies. That collection is controlled by those platforms, not by us.

8. Children’s privacy

Our apps are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above. Because we collect no personal data, we cannot notify you individually.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at mstevens@dqbbs.com.
