Privacy Policy

The short version: this app collects nothing.

Last updated: 28 July 2026

In plain English: Apps No Ads apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information. They contain no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers, and they work fully offline. Anything you create in an app stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Apps No Ads — the software label of Melvin Stevens — handles information in its applications and on this website (appsnoads.com). It applies to Simply Sudoku and all other Apps No Ads apps.

1. Information collected

None. These apps do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information. There is no account to create and no sign-in. No advertising identifiers are used.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the apps work, some information is saved locally on your device only and is never transmitted to Apps No Ads or anyone else. For Simply Sudoku this includes:

  • Your current game and puzzle progress;
  • Your best completion times per difficulty;
  • App settings and preferences.

This data stays in the app’s private storage on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

3. No ads, analytics, or tracking

These apps contain no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no social-media or third-party tracking libraries. No user profiles are built, and there is nothing to sell to third parties, because nothing is collected.

4. No internet connection required

Simply Sudoku is designed to run fully offline. It does not send data over the network during normal use.

5. Donations

If you choose to make a voluntary donation, the payment is processed by Stripe, a third-party payment provider, through a secure Stripe checkout page. Apps No Ads never sees or stores your card details. Any information you provide there is handled under Stripe’s Privacy Policy. Donations are optional and are not required to use any app.

6. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads no third-party fonts or trackers. The web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliability; these are not used to identify or track you.

7. App store platforms

The app stores that distribute these apps (Google Play, the Apple App Store, and the Microsoft Store) may collect their own information — for example, download counts or aggregate statistics — under their own privacy policies. That collection is controlled by those platforms, not by Apps No Ads.

8. Children’s privacy

These apps are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above revised. Because no personal data is collected, individual notifications aren’t possible.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email mstevens@dqbbs.com.
