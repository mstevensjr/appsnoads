The philosophy

Software should not frustrate you.

Most apps are built to capture your attention and sell it — which is why they inundate you with ads, pop-ups, upsells, and trackers. Apps No Ads exists to do the opposite: to make genuinely useful, appealing apps that are fast and hassle-free, then get out of your way. You install it, it works, and nothing interrupts you. That’s the whole idea behind everything here.

  • No advertisements, ever
  • No subscriptions — a paid app is paid once
  • No in-app purchases
  • No nagging or pop-ups
  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No internet connection required
  • Small, fast, and battery-friendly

The #1 way to help — bigger than a coffee

Please rate & review the apps you use

A quick rating and review is the single most powerful thing you can do to help — and it’s completely free.

Apps No Ads apps have no ads and no advertising budget. They can’t pay to appear on the app stores — they only get noticed when people like you leave a review. Every rating pushes an app higher in search, helps new people discover honest, ad-free software, and keeps it growing without spending a cent on ads. Great reviews are the advertising.

  • Takes about 30 seconds
  • 100% free — the biggest way to help
  • Helps others find an ad-free app
