The philosophy

Software should not frustrate you.

Most apps are built to capture your attention and sell it — which is why they inundate you with ads, pop-ups, upsells, and trackers. Apps No Ads exists to do the opposite: to make genuinely useful, appealing apps that are fast and hassle-free, then get out of your way. You install it, it works, and nothing interrupts you. That’s the whole idea behind everything here, starting with Simply Sudoku.

  • No advertisements, ever
  • No subscriptions or paywalls
  • No in-app purchases
  • No nagging or pop-ups
  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No internet connection required
  • Small, fast, and battery-friendly
