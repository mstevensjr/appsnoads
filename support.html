Frequently asked questions

Is Simply Sudoku really free, with no ads?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls, and no in-app purchases required to play. Donations are welcome but entirely optional.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. The app works completely offline and there is no sign-up or login. You install it and play.

Does the app collect or share my data?

No. I collect nothing. Your current game, best times, and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. See my Privacy Policy for details.

Which platforms is it available on?

Simply Sudoku is built to run on Android, iOS, and Windows. Store links will appear on my download page as each version goes live.

Are the puzzles really unique?

Yes. Every puzzle is generated and verified to have exactly one solution, so you can always solve by logic alone — no guessing required.

How do notes, hints, and undo work?

Tap the pencil to toggle notes mode and pencil-in candidates. Hints reveal a correct digit (a limited number per difficulty). Undo and redo let you step back and forward through your moves without penalty.

My progress or best time disappeared. What happened?

Progress and best times are saved locally on your device. Clearing the app’s data, uninstalling, or moving to a new device will remove them, since nothing is stored in the cloud. If something looks wrong, please email me.

How can I support the app?

The best ways: leave a nice review on your app store, tell others and spread the word, and — if you’d like — make a small donation. Can’t donate right now? No problem at all — a review, or even a quick email to say you appreciate Simply Sudoku, means just as much. Every bit helps me keep building ad-free software.

Still need help?

I welcome any and all feedback. Because I don’t collect any information about you, I have no way to reach you first — so if you have a bug report, a suggestion, or just a kind word, please email me. I read every message and I’ll get back to you.
